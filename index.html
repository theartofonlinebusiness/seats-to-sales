Your budget decides how many ad sets you can run. Each ad set needs about 7 registrants a day (about 50 a week) for Meta to learn, and each ad needs about $10 a day, with no more than 6 ads in one ad set.

Terms

Cost per registrant. Your ad spend divided by the people who signed up. On a paid event, this is your cost per purchase.

Earnings per registrant. Your total launch sales divided by everyone who registered. This is your right hand.

Your ceiling. Half your earnings per registrant. Above this, an ad is costing you more than it's likely to earn.

Trend friend. Today, yesterday and the two days before. Is the cost getting more expensive, holding steady, or getting cheaper? Act on the trend, never on one day.

Control ad. Your best ad right now. It stays on, untouched, until a new ad beats it for 2 to 3 days.

Warm audiences. Your email list, customer list, website visitors (180 days), leads (180 days), Instagram followers, Facebook followers and video viewers (ThruPlay, 180 days).
